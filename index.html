Turning single-use wrappers into premium sheets & furniture

Plascycle converts **Multi-Layered Plastics (MLPs)** and food wrappers into durable plies, sheets and furniture — bringing value back to waste while creating jobs and **saving trees**.

Prototype

Value

From waste to valuable sheets and furniture.

Vision

Scale up and create a circular economy.
